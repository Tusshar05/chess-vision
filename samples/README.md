# Sample photos

Six photos from the **test split** (ChessReD2K) of the Chess Recognition Dataset (ChessReD), resized to 1280 px.

- Source: Masouris & van Gemert, *End-to-End Chess Recognition*, VISAPP 2024. 4TU.ResearchData, DOI 10.4121/99b5c721-280b-450b-b058-b2900b69a90f.v2
- License: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (as listed by 4TU.ResearchData) - attribution, non-commercial, share-alike. These resized copies are shared under the same license.
- `labels.json`: ground-truth piece placement (FEN field 1) per photo.
